Data Usage

The app needs “Usage access” permission to read the traffic counters.
The counter resets every day at 00:00 (device time).
Drag the red and green lines to select a period. A dashed line means the monitor was not recording during that time. Use the checkboxes to show or hide a group in the chart and the table.
Note: Wi-Fi totals include local-network traffic (for example an HFS server), because Android does not separate it from internet traffic. The “Local network” group only contains apps that you assign to it below.

Apps (Wi-Fi)

Billing cycle

At 00:00 on that day the finished cycle is archived with all its data and a new one starts from zero. The whole history is kept; it is only split into cycles. If a month is shorter than the chosen day, the cycle starts on its last day.

Background recording

Silent mode (default): the counters are read about every 15 minutes in the background and every time you open the app, with no notification. Totals stay exact because they come from Android's own counters; only the position of points on the chart can be a little coarser when the phone is idle. Turn it off for precise mode: a foreground service reads the counters every 30 seconds and Android shows a minimised notification for it.

Backup

Export saves the full history, app groups and cycle day to a JSON file. Import merges a backup into the current history (the larger value wins for each record), so it is safe to import after reinstalling.
